Check what you can get free first

Your local council can arrange a free occupational therapist (OT) assessment, and many aids such as grab rails, toilet frames, bath boards and perching stools are often provided or fitted free after one. Contact your council's adult social care team, or ask your GP. Equipment for health needs can also come through the NHS.
