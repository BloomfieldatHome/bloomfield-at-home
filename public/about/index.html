About Bloomfield at Home

Bloomfield at Home is an independent guide site. It brings together plain-English information about the practical side of later life at home: alarms and home safety, mobility equipment, wills and Lasting Powers of Attorney, everyday aids and household bills.

What we are not

We are not a care provider and we do not give medical, legal or financial advice. Bloomfield at Home is separate from Bloomfield Care, a home care provider. Bloomfield Care's care staff do not recommend or sell products to the people they support.

If you are looking for home care or live-in care, you can find Bloomfield Care at bloomfield-care.com.

How we choose what to include

  • We show free and official routes first, such as council assessments and gov.uk services.
  • We only list established UK providers with clear terms.
  • We do not cover insurance, funeral plans, pensions, equity release, loans or health supplements.
  • Some links earn us a commission. They are always labelled. See how we make money.

Spotted a mistake?

Rules and prices change. If something looks out of date, email hello@bloomfieldathome.co.uk and we will check it.
